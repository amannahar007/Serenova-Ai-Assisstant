# Privacy Policy for Serenova AI

**Last updated:** September 27, 2026  
**Effective date:** September 27, 2026

Serenova AI ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your personal privacy and providing transparent disclosures regarding data usage and hardware permissions. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled when you use the Serenova AI mobile application and associated services.

---

### 1. Camera Usage & On-Device Facial Expression Analysis
- **Purpose:** The App provides an optional, experimental facial expression check-in feature that classifies basic outward facial expressions (e.g., happy, sad, surprised, neutral).
- **On-Device Processing:** Camera video streams are analyzed strictly **in real-time on your device** using client-side machine learning models.
- **Zero Storage / Zero Transmission:** No photographs, video streams, or biometric templates are ever recorded, saved, stored on device storage, or transmitted to our servers or third parties.
- **Non-Diagnostic Nature:** This feature is **not** a medical device, diagnosis, mood detector, lie detector, or clinical tool. It does not measure anxiety, depression, stress, fatigue, or heart rate.

---

### 2. Microphone & Voice Input
- **Purpose:** When you tap the microphone button, the App requests microphone access solely to transcribe your spoken voice into text for chat prompts using speech recognition.
- **Audio Handling:** Spoken audio is processed in real time strictly for speech-to-text transcription. Raw audio files and recordings are never recorded, saved, or uploaded to our permanent servers.

---

### 3. Chat Data & AI Processing (Gemini API)
- **Conversational Content:** Messages and chat history you choose to submit are transmitted via secure HTTPS encryption to our backend and Google Gemini API services strictly to generate relevant AI conversational responses.
- **Data Retention & Control:** Chat sessions are synchronized with your authenticated Firebase account so you can access conversation history across devices. You have the ability to clear or remove your chat sessions at any time.

---

### 4. Account & Authentication Information
- **User Authentication:** When you create an account or sign in, we process basic account credentials (such as email address, display name, and profile avatar if provided via Firebase Authentication) to manage your user profile and maintain your session.

---

### 5. Payments & Subscriptions
- **Android In-App Purchases:** For digital subscriptions on Android, all transactions are processed securely through **Google Play In-App Billing**. We do not collect or store your payment card numbers, bank details, or UPI credentials.
- **Account Entitlement:** Google Play provides a cryptographically signed purchase token that our server validates to grant active subscription tier access to your Serenova account.

---

### 6. Data Security
- All communications between the Serenova application, Firebase, and backend servers use industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) encryption in transit.

---

### 7. Children's Privacy
- Serenova AI is not directed to individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
